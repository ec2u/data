# 2024-11-11 – AI extraction

Units listed by AI from the research structure directories, then profiled by AI from their websites and linked pages,
or from the listing directory where a unit has no website. A unit is profiled again only once that page changes; units
the directories no longer list are retracted, unless a run would leave fewer than three quarters of them in place.
