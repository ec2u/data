---
title: Webmasters
---

The EC2U Knowledge Hub harvests content from the websites of EC2U universities and their local partners. As a web
designer or site manager, you decide how that content is structured and where it lives. Those choices set how completely
and reliably the hub can carry your content to the whole alliance and keep it up to date.

# Structuring Content

The [data models](/models/) list the properties each dataset can hold, such as titles, dates, locations,
languages and audiences. State each of them as a distinct, consistently placed element on your pages, rather than inside
free text, so the hub can read each property reliably and show it in the right place.

Keep a stable address for every programme, course, document and event, and a listing page or feed that links to all of
them. The hub follows these listings to find new content and to retire what is no longer published.

# Changing Your Site

The hub depends on the addresses and page structure of each source it harvests. Before you move, redesign or retire a
section that the hub reads, open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ec2u/data/issues) so the harvest
can be adapted in time and your content stays on the hub without interruption or gaps.

Report missing or wrongly mapped content from your site as an issue on GitHub too, naming the page it was taken from and
the detail that went wrong, so the harvest of that page can be checked and fixed.
