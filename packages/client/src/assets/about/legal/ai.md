# How the Hub Uses AI

Much of the content on the EC2U Knowledge Hub is produced automatically. Crawlers gather information from partner
universities and other sources, then large language models process it in three ways:

- **Extraction** – AI turns free text into structured data, such as dates, places, descriptions and summaries
- **Classification** – AI assigns resources to the topics, levels and types of the shared taxonomies
- **Translation** – AI renders titles and descriptions in English where the source states them in another language

Every resource that AI generated, classified or translated in whole or in part carries an **AI-Generated Content**
badge, which links to this notice and sits next to a button for reporting errors in it. The API marks the same resources
with the `generated` property. A resource without the badge was gathered from its source as published: AI added,
classified or translated nothing in it, so it reads exactly as its source states it.

# Using AI Content Responsibly

> [!CAUTION]
>
> **Do not rely on AI-generated content alone for important decisions.** It may contain errors, be incomplete or out
> of date, or even be fabricated. Always verify important information with official sources before you act on it.

Treat automated content as a starting point, not as the final word. Check the original source wherever a resource links
to one, and report any problem you find, so that it can be corrected for everyone else.

# Accuracy and Corrections

Automated content is published without prior review of each item, so the hub cannot guarantee that it is accurate,
complete or up to date. The University of Pavia remains responsible for the operation of the hub: it corrects or removes
content found to be wrong once reported, and may update or remove automated content at any time.

# Feedback and Questions

Reports of errors in automated content show which tasks need fixing first and make the hub more reliable for everyone.

- **Errors** – select **Report AI-generated content** on the page showing the error, or open a [GitHub issue][issues]
- **Questions** – write to the EC2U Connect Centre at [cc@ml.ec2u.eu](mailto:cc@ml.ec2u.eu)
- **Security issues** – report them privately through [GitHub security](https://github.com/ec2u/data/security)
