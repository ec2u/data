# Commitment

The University of Pavia runs the EC2U Knowledge Hub and wants everyone to be able to use it, whatever their abilities
or the device they browse with. The standard we work towards is level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
(WCAG) 2.1, as required of public bodies by Italian Law 4/2004 and by Directive (EU) 2016/2102.

# Status

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> **This notice is provisional.** The University of Pavia has not yet filed the formal accessibility statement of the
> hub with AgID, the Agency for Digital Italy. This page will link to the statement as soon as it is published.

The hub has not yet been formally assessed against the standard, so we cannot say which of its parts already meet it.

# Known Limitations

Much of the hub's content is gathered automatically from partner universities and other sources, and enriched with AI.
That content may lack text alternatives for images, or be shown in a language other than the one your browser asks for.

# Feedback

If something on the hub is hard or impossible for you to use, tell us the address of the page and what went wrong.
Mention the browser and any assistive technology you were using, as that helps us reproduce the problem.

- **By email**: write to the EC2U Connect Centre at [cc@ml.ec2u.eu](mailto:cc@ml.ec2u.eu)
- **On GitHub**: open an issue at [github.com/ec2u/data/issues](https://github.com/ec2u/data/issues)
