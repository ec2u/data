# Commitment

The University of Pavia runs the EC2U Knowledge Hub and wants everyone to be able to use it, whatever their abilities or
the device they browse with. The hub works towards the harmonised standard EN 301 549, which adopts level AA of the Web
Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, as required of public bodies by Italian Law 4/2004, by Directive (EU)
2016/2102 and by the accessibility guidelines of AgID, the Agency for Digital Italy.

# Status

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> **This notice is provisional.** The University of Pavia has not yet filed the formal accessibility statement of the
> hub with AgID. This page will link to the statement as soon as it is published.

The hub has not yet been formally assessed against the standard, so it is not yet known which of its parts meet it.

# Limitations

Content gathered automatically from partner universities and other sources may lack text alternatives for images, or
appear in a language other than the one your browser requests, even after enrichment with AI.

# Feedback

If something on the hub is hard or impossible for you to use, report the address of the page and what went wrong.
Mention the browser and any assistive technology you were using, as that helps reproduce the problem.

- **On the hub** – select the [feedback](../feedback) button of the page and choose the **Bug** type
- **On GitHub** – open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/ec2u/data/issues)
- **By email** – write to the EC2U Connect Centre at [cc@ml.ec2u.eu](mailto:cc@ml.ec2u.eu)

# Enforcement

If you receive no answer within 30 days, or an answer you find unsatisfactory, you can turn to the digital ombudsman of
AgID, the [Difensore civico per il digitale][ombudsman], as provided by article 3-quinquies of Law 4/2004.
