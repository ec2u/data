# Status

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> **This notice is provisional.** The University of Pavia has not yet filed the formal accessibility statement of the
> EC2U Knowledge Hub with AgID, the Italian digital agency. This page will link to the statement once it is published.

The University of Pavia runs the hub and wants everyone to be able to use it, whatever their abilities or the device
they browse with. The standard we work towards is level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, as
required of public bodies by Italian Law 4/2004 and by Directive (EU) 2016/2102.

# Known Limitations

The hub has not yet been formally assessed against that standard, so we can't tell you which parts already meet it.

Much of its content is gathered automatically from partner universities and other sources, and enriched with AI. That
content may lack text alternatives for images, or be shown in a language other than the one your browser asks for.

# Feedback

If something on the hub is hard or impossible for you to use, tell us the address of the page and what went wrong.

- **By email**: write to the EC2U Connect Centre at [cc@ml.ec2u.eu](mailto:cc@ml.ec2u.eu)
- **On GitHub**: open an issue at [github.com/ec2u/data/issues](https://github.com/ec2u/data/issues)
