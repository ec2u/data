# Content

## Data Reuse

Each dataset states its licence on its info page, reached through the info icon in the page menu: taxonomies, for
instance, use [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0][cc]. Reuse a dataset under its licence, and credit the EC2U Alliance as its publisher.

Content gathered from partner universities and other sources remains subject to the rights of its original publishers.
Follow the source links provided with each resource to check those rights before reusing the content. If you hold
rights in content shown on the hub and want it corrected or removed, write to the EC2U Connect Centre.

## AI Content

The hub gathers much of its content automatically and enriches it with AI. See [AI Content Notice](ai) for which content
is generated, how it is marked, how far you can rely on it, and how we handle its errors.

## Software

The software running the hub is open source. Reuse its code from [GitHub][code] under the [Apache License 2.0][apache].

# Visitors

## Privacy

The hub needs no account, sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics service. See [Privacy Notice](privacy) for
what it records about its visitors and about the people named in its datasets, how long it keeps that information,
and how to see, correct or remove the data it holds about you, or to object to the way the hub uses it.

## Accessibility

The hub aims to be usable by everyone. See [Accessibility Notice](accessibility) for the standard we work towards, the
limitations we know of, and how to tell us about anything that is hard or impossible for you to use.
