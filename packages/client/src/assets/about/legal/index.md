# Content

## Data Reuse

Each dataset states the terms it is published under on its info page, reached through the info icon in the page menu.
Taxonomies, for instance, are released under [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).
Reuse a dataset within the terms of its licence, and credit the EC2U Alliance as its publisher.

Content gathered from partner universities and other sources remains subject to the rights of its original publishers.
Follow the source links provided with each resource to check those rights before reusing the content.

## AI Content

The hub gathers much of its content automatically and enriches it with AI. See [AI Content Notice](ai) for which content
is generated, how far you can rely on it, and where our responsibility for its errors ends.

## Software

The software running the hub is open source. Its code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ec2u/data), and you
may reuse it under the terms of the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

# Visitors

## Privacy

The hub needs no account and sets no tracking cookies. See [Privacy Notice](privacy) for what its servers record when
you visit, how long they keep it, and how to have data about you corrected or removed.

## Accessibility

The hub aims to be usable by everyone. See [Accessibility Notice](accessibility) for the standard we work towards, the
limitations we know of, and how to tell us about anything that is hard or impossible for you to use.
