# Reuse

## Records

The datasets of the hub are catalogues: each record describes a research unit, a degree program, a course or another
resource held by a partner university or an external source, and links to it. The copyright and licence stated on the
info page of a dataset, reached through the info icon in the page menu, cover these records only. Reuse the records
under that licence, currently [CC BY-NC-ND 4.0][cc] for every dataset, and credit the EC2U Alliance as their source.

## Sources

The copyright and licence of a dataset never extend to the content its records describe, which stays subject to the
rights of its original publishers: follow the source link of each resource to check those rights before reusing it. If
you hold rights in content shown on the hub and want it corrected or removed, write to the EC2U Connect Centre.

## Software

The software running the hub is open source. Reuse its code from [GitHub][code] under the [Apache License 2.0][apache].

# Notices

## AI Content

The hub gathers much of its content automatically and enriches it with AI. See the [AI Content Notice](ai) for which
content is generated, how it is marked, how far it can be relied on and how its errors are corrected or removed.

## Accessibility

The hub aims to be usable by everyone. See the [Accessibility Notice](accessibility) for the standard the hub works
towards, the limitations known so far, and how to report anything that is hard or impossible for you to use.

## Privacy

The hub needs no account, sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics service. See the [Privacy Notice](privacy) for
what it records about its visitors and about the people named in its datasets, how long it keeps that information, and
how to see, correct or remove the data it holds about you, or to object to the way the hub uses it.
