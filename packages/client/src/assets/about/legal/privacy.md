# Service

**Name of the Service**

EC2U Knowledge Hub - University of Pavia

# Description

**Description of the Service**

The EC2U Knowledge Hub is the central knowledge sharing and semantic search facility of the EC2U Alliance. It gathers
content from EC2U universities and external sources, and publishes it as cross-linked datasets on areas of common
interest, such as research units, degree programs and courses. The hub needs no account and has no authenticated users.

# Jurisdiction

Processing is subject to Italian and EU data protection law and supervised by the Italian Data Protection Authority.

# Personal Data

**Personal Data Processed**

1. The following data is recorded when you browse the hub or call its API:

	- your IP address
	- the time of each request
	- the address requested
	- the browser you use

1. The following data is gathered from public pages of the partner universities and other public sources:

	- the name of people mentioned in a public professional role, such as unit contacts, instructors or event organisers
	- their professional contact details, where the source page publishes them

1. The following data is gathered from yourself:

	- your name, email address and message, if you write to the EC2U Connect Centre
	- the same data, if you send feedback through the [feedback page](../feedback), along with the type you choose, the
		page you write about and, for a bug report, your browser and your operating system

	 The feedback page sends nothing on its own: it prepares an email, which you review and send from your own account.

The hub sets no tracking or advertising cookies and uses no analytics service. Your browser may store your language and
display preferences locally: they never leave your device and stay there until you clear the data of the site.

Issues opened on GitHub are public and processed by GitHub under its own privacy terms.

# Purpose

**Purpose of the Processing of Personal Data**

Technical records of your requests serve only to keep the service secure and running.

Messages sent to the EC2U Connect Centre or through the feedback page serve only to answer them and to fix the problems
they report in the hub, in its datasets or in the content gathered from its sources.

Information about people named in datasets supports the search and discovery of the courses, research, events and other
activities of the EC2U universities, as described by the public pages that the information is gathered from.

# Third Parties

**Third Parties to Whom Personal Data Is Disclosed**

Personal data is disclosed to third parties only where the law requires it, for instance at the request of an authority.

The hub runs on Google Cloud, which acts as processor on behalf of the University of Pavia. Google Cloud hosts the hub,
keeps its technical records and runs the AI models that process gathered content. All personal data the hub handles is
processed and stored within a single Google Cloud region in the European Union, and is never used to train AI models.

Datasets are public: information about people named in them is accessible to anyone who browses the hub or calls its
API, as it already is on the public pages of the universities it was gathered from.

# Your Rights

**How to Access, Rectify and Delete the Personal Data**

The EC2U Knowledge Hub collects no personal information from its visitors beyond the technical records and messages
listed above, and it deletes the technical records at the end of the period stated under [Retention](#retention).

Information about people named in datasets is gathered from public pages by automated tools, and AI may extract or
translate it, which can introduce errors, as explained in the [AI Content Notice](ai). Each resource links back to the
page it was gathered from, so you can always check it against the original. To rectify information about you at its
source, contact the university or organisation that publishes that page: the hub takes up the change at its next update.

To access, rectify or delete information the hub holds about you, or to restrict or object to its use, write to the Data
Protection Officer named under [Data Controller](#data-controller), quoting the address of the resource concerned.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, by writing to
[protocollo@gpdp.it](mailto:protocollo@gpdp.it) or to [protocollo@pec.gpdp.it](mailto:protocollo@pec.gpdp.it).

# Retention

**Data Retention**

Technical records are kept for 30 days, then deleted automatically.

Information about people named in datasets is kept as long as the source it was gathered from publishes it. The hub
removes it at the first update after the source withdraws it, or earlier at your request.
