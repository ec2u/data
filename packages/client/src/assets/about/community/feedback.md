title: Providing Feedback
---

Every page of the hub but the home page carries a feedback button, marked by a speech bubble icon. It sits at the bottom
left of the side column, or of the menu drawer on phones. Readers use it to report a problem, suggest an improvement or
ask a question about the page they are reading. No GitHub account or other registration is required.

On pages showing AI-generated content, the button turns red and opens the feedback page with **AI Content** already
chosen as type. It then sits at the end of the bar, next to an **AI-Generated Content** notice. The notice links to the
[AI Content Notice](../legal/ai), which explains how such content is produced and why it may be wrong.

# Sending Feedback

1. Select **Provide feedback about this page** on the page you want to write about: the [feedback page](/about/feedback)
	 opens in a new tab naming that page, so that your feedback reaches the hub team with its address; opened directly,
	 the feedback page takes feedback on the hub as a whole rather than on a single page of it
1. Choose the **Type** that best fits your feedback:
	- **General**: any comment on the hub
	- **AI Content**: wrong or misleading AI-generated content
	- **Data Error**: wrong or outdated content
	- **Bug**: a page or feature that does not work as expected
	- **Suggestion**: a feature or source the hub should offer
	- **Question**: anything about the hub or its data
1. Check the **Subject**: it names the page you came from and cannot be changed; on a feedback page opened directly,
	 state in a few words what your feedback is about, as the subject heads the email the hub team receives
1. Describe your feedback in the **Message** field, then fill in the detail fields of the chosen type, if any:
	- **Bug**: the **Steps to Reproduce**, the **Expected Behaviour** and the **Actual Behaviour** are required, while
		**Notes** may add anything else that could help the hub team track the problem down
	- **AI Content**: optionally, the **Wrong Content** as shown on the page and the **Expected Content** it should read
1. Select **Submit**, available once the subject, the message and any required details are filled in: your email client
   opens with the feedback ready to send to the hub team, and the page confirms that the feedback is ready
1. Review the email and send it from your EC2U partner university account
1. Select **Dismiss** to close the feedback tab and go back to the page you were reading

# What to Know

- The email carries the subject, the type, the message, any details filled in and the page it is about; a bug report
	also carries the browser and the operating system, as described in the [Privacy Notice](../legal/privacy)
- The feedback page sends and stores nothing on its own: nothing reaches the hub team until you send the email
- Errors in the content of a source page are best corrected on the source site itself: the next scheduled harvest
	carries the correction to the hub, where it appears after a delay with no feedback needed
