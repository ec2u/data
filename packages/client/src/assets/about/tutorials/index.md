title: Tutorials
---

The tutorials introduce the concepts needed to understand and reuse the datasets of the hub. They address
researchers, developers and analysts who want to explore the data or integrate it into their own applications. Each
tutorial starts from first principles and builds up to the models, vocabularies and access methods the hub relies on.
No prior knowledge of linked data is assumed, so readers new to the field can follow them in sequence.

- [Linked Data Data Models](/about/tutorials/linked-data/) — ontologies, taxonomies and graph-based data models
