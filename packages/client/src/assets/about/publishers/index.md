title: Publishers
---

The EC2U Knowledge Hub gathers content owned by EC2U universities and their local partners: offices, departments and
services that publish programmes, courses, documents and events on their own sites. As a publisher, you decide which of
your content the hub carries and you answer for its accuracy. The hub harmonises that content and shares it with the
whole alliance, always linking back to your own pages as the authoritative source.

# Publishing on the Hub

To propose a new source, or to change how an existing one is harvested, open an issue on the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/ec2u/data/issues). State the dataset the content belongs to, the address where it
is published, the languages it is available in and how often it is updated, along with a contact for follow-up.

Each source is harvested on a schedule, mapped to a shared data model and cross-linked with the content of every other
source. The [data models](/about/models/) list the properties each dataset can hold: check them to see which of
your content the hub can carry and which details it can show to users across the alliance.

# Keeping Content Accurate

The hub mirrors your published content, so a correction made at the source reaches the hub on the next harvest. Report
errors the hub itself introduces, such as a wrong mapping or a missing resource, as an issue on GitHub.

Crawlers and AI enrichment may introduce errors or gaps. See [AI Content Notice](../legal/ai) for how AI is used
on the hub, what it adds to the content you publish, and why its output may differ from your original wording.

# Rights

Content gathered from your sources remains subject to your rights as its original publisher. Each resource on the hub
links back to the page it was taken from. See [Legal Notice](../legal/) for the terms the hub applies to reuse.
