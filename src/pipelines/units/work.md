# Summary

The Department of Biology and Biotechnology "L. Spallanzani" (DBB) is a dynamic academic unit recognized as a "
Department of Excellence." It conducts advanced research across a broad spectrum of biological and biotechnological
fields, ranging from molecular and cellular biology to genomics, neurobiology, and environmental applications. The
department is committed to scientific progress, innovation, and public engagement, fostering collaborations with
industry and actively participating in knowledge transfer initiatives.

# General Description

The Department of Biology and Biotechnology "L. Spallanzani" (DBB) is a distinguished research and educational
institution. It is recognized as a "Department of Excellence" for its significant contributions to the biological
sciences and biotechnology. The department is dedicated to advancing scientific knowledge through cutting-edge research
and to training future generations of scientists.

The DBB's research activities span a wide array of disciplines, encompassing fundamental biological processes and their
applications in various sectors. The department emphasizes interdisciplinary approaches, leveraging advanced
technologies and methodologies to address complex scientific challenges. Beyond its research endeavors, the DBB is
actively involved in innovation and public engagement, promoting the dissemination of scientific results and fostering
collaborations with industrial partners to translate research into tangible benefits for society.
