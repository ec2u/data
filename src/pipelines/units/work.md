# Summary

The Department of Biology and Biotechnology "Lazzaro Spallanzani" (DBB) is a dynamic research unit focused on advancing
scientific knowledge in biological and biotechnological fields. It conducts interdisciplinary research across various
areas, including molecular biology, genetics, neuroscience, pharmacology, and environmental biology. The department is
committed to both fundamental and applied research, aiming to contribute to national and international scientific
progress and address contemporary challenges in health, agriculture, and sustainability.

# General Description

The Department of Biology and Biotechnology "Lazzaro Spallanzani" (DBB) is a vibrant academic and research institution
dedicated to excellence in biological and biotechnological sciences. Located in Pavia, Italy, the department fosters an
environment of scientific inquiry and innovation, engaging in a broad spectrum of research activities that span from
basic molecular mechanisms to their applications in various sectors. The DBB is recognized for its contributions to
understanding complex biological systems and developing novel biotechnological solutions.

The department's research endeavors are characterized by a multidisciplinary approach, integrating diverse fields such
as bioinformatics, genomics, cell biology, and neurobiology. It aims to unravel fundamental biological processes,
investigate the molecular bases of human diseases, and explore biotechnological applications in areas like agriculture
and environmental sustainability. The DBB is also committed to training future generations of scientists, offering
comprehensive educational programs in biological and biotechnological sciences.

The DBB actively participates in national and international research programs and collaborations, striving to translate
scientific discoveries into tangible benefits for society. Its research infrastructure supports advanced studies in
areas such as ancient DNA analysis, structural biology, and microbial genetics. The department's commitment to public
engagement ensures the dissemination of research findings and the valorization of knowledge for broader societal impact.
