# Summary

The Department of Civil Engineering and Architecture (DICAr) is a research unit dedicated to advancing knowledge in
civil engineering and architecture. Its research focuses on four key multidisciplinary areas: protecting territories and
communities from natural and environmental risks, the sustainable conservation and valorization of built heritage,
virtual modeling and additive manufacturing for advanced materials, and the development of smart sustainable cities.
DICAr emphasizes innovative technologies and methodologies, fostering collaborations with industries and contributing to
technological transfer through spin-offs, startups, and patents. The department also houses a large experimental
laboratory for testing materials and structures, particularly renowned in seismic engineering.

# General Description

The Department of Civil Engineering and Architecture (DICAr) is a prominent academic and research institution committed
to addressing contemporary challenges in the built and natural environments. The department's research activities are
structured around four core multidisciplinary themes, reflecting a comprehensive approach to engineering and
architectural innovation. These themes include the protection of territories, communities, and assets from natural and
environmental hazards, focusing on risk assessment, vulnerability reduction, and emergency management.

A significant area of focus for DICAr is the knowledge, conservation, reuse, and sustainable enhancement of built
heritage. This involves developing strategies for preserving historical structures and promoting sustainable practices
within the existing urban fabric. Furthermore, the department is at the forefront of virtual modeling and additive
manufacturing, particularly 3D printing, for advanced materials, aiming to combine academic and industrial research to
create innovative solutions and reduce the need for extensive experimental testing.

DICAr also dedicates substantial effort to the concept of the smart sustainable city, developing technologies for data
acquisition and interdisciplinary methodologies for urban analysis. This work supports policy planning related to
environmental sustainability, safety, resilience, equity, and social cohesion. The department boasts one of Italy's
largest university experimental laboratories, specializing in testing materials and structures, and is recognized as a
leading European center for seismic engineering research, often collaborating with the adjacent EUCentre.

The department actively promotes technological transfer, translating its scientific expertise into practical
applications through the creation of spin-offs, startups, and patents. It also fosters strong synergies with industries,
leading to new collaborative projects that often incorporate the digitalization of processes and products. This
integrated approach ensures that DICAr's research not only advances academic knowledge but also contributes directly to
societal and industrial progress.

# Summary

The Department of Chemistry promotes and coordinates research across various fields of chemistry, offering bachelor's
and master's degree programs in Chemistry, and participating in the PhD School of Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences
and Industrial Innovation. Its research activities span fundamental and applied chemistry, including analytical,
physical, inorganic, and organic chemistry, with a focus on interdisciplinary areas such as agriculture, climate change,

# General Description

The Department of Chemistry is a prominent academic unit dedicated to fostering scientific research and education within
the broad spectrum of chemical sciences. Located at the University of Pavia, the department plays a crucial role in
advancing knowledge through its comprehensive research programs and by training future generations of chemists. It is
structured to support diverse scientific-disciplinary areas, including analytical, physical, inorganic, and organic
chemistry, which form the core of its investigative pursuits.

Beyond traditional chemical disciplines, the department actively engages in interdisciplinary research that addresses
pressing global issues. Key areas of focus include the application of chemical principles to agriculture, contributing
to sustainable practices and food security. Furthermore, the department conducts significant research related to climate
change, exploring innovative chemical solutions and understanding environmental impacts.

The department also specializes in the development of advanced materials and energy solutions, with a strong emphasis on
energy and materials chemistry. This involves the creation of novel substances and processes for various technological
applications. Additionally, a notable area of expertise lies in industrial nanobiotechnologies for pharmaceuticals,
where researchers leverage nanotechnology to develop new diagnostic tools and therapeutic agents, bridging chemistry
with biology and medicine for pharmaceutical innovation.

Through its commitment to both fundamental and applied research, the Department of Chemistry aims to contribute to
scientific progress and societal well-being. It fosters collaborations with national and international partners,
ensuring its research remains at the forefront of chemical science and its applications.

# Summary

The Department of Law at the University of Pavia conducts innovative research and projects of national and European
relevance across various legal fields. Its activities involve faculty, research fellows, scholarship holders, and PhD
students, often in collaboration with departmental and interdepartmental research centers. Key research areas include
constitutional law, digital law, health law, criminal procedure, philosophy of law, labor law, international law, and
governance. The department also hosts the Center for Law and Health Studies, focusing on the legal implications within
the healthcare sector through a multidisciplinary and international approach.

# General Description

The Department of Law at the University of Pavia is a distinguished academic unit with a rich historical tradition
dating back to the university's founding in 1361. Today, it continues to advance legal scholarship through innovative
teaching and research methodologies. The department is committed to conducting research and projects that hold
significant national and European relevance, fostering an environment of intellectual inquiry and collaboration.

Research activities within the Department of Law span a broad spectrum of legal disciplines. These include foundational
areas such as constitutional law, philosophy of law, and international law, alongside specialized fields like digital
law, health law, and labor law. The department also explores contemporary issues such as territorial governance,
criminal procedure, and the legal aspects of organized crime in the digital age.

A notable initiative within the department is the Center for Law and Health Studies, established in 2023. This center is
dedicated to high-level research and training in health law, addressing the complex legal challenges within the evolving
healthcare sector. It adopts an integrated, multidisciplinary approach, drawing insights from legal, economic,
sociological, and medical sciences, and emphasizes international collaboration to provide a comprehensive understanding
of health-related legal issues.

The Department of Law actively engages in scientific activities through its faculty, research fellows, scholarship
holders, and PhD students. It also collaborates with numerous departmental and interdepartmental research centers,
enhancing its capacity for impactful research and contributing to the broader academic community.

# Summary

The Department of Humanities at the University of Pavia conducts innovative research and projects of national and
European significance. It builds upon a long-standing humanistic tradition, integrating it with modern cultural demands.
The department is structured into several sections, including Philosophy, Foreign Languages and Literatures, Theoretical
and Applied Linguistics, Sciences of Antiquity, Sciences of Literature, Art, and Performing Arts, and Historical and
Geographical Sciences. Its scientific activities are supported by collaborations with numerous departmental and
interdepartmental research centers, contributing to a prominent profile in the Italian and international university
landscape.

# General Description

The Department of Humanities at the University of Pavia was established in 2013, continuing the distinguished and
centuries-old humanistic legacy of the former Faculty of Letters and Philosophy. The department is dedicated to
fostering advanced research and innovative projects that hold both national and European relevance. Its academic
endeavors are driven by the commitment of its faculty, alongside the active participation of research fellows,
scholarship holders, and doctoral students.

The department's scientific activities are significantly enhanced through collaborations with various departmental and
interdepartmental research centers. This collaborative environment supports a broad spectrum of research interests,
ensuring a dynamic and interdisciplinary approach to humanistic studies. The department's structure is organized into
specialized sections, each focusing on distinct yet interconnected fields of inquiry.

These sections include Philosophy, which explores fundamental questions of existence and knowledge; Foreign Languages
and Literatures, dedicated to the study of diverse linguistic and literary traditions; and Theoretical and Applied
Linguistics, which investigates the structure and use of language. The Sciences of Antiquity section delves into
classical archaeology, Etruscology, classical philology, ancient history, and oriental studies, offering a comprehensive
understanding of ancient civilizations.

Further enriching its research profile, the department encompasses the Sciences of Literature, Art, and Performing Arts,
covering literature, philology, linguistics, art, and visual and performing arts. Lastly, the Historical and
Geographical Sciences section examines historical events and geographical phenomena. Through its diverse research areas
and commitment to scholarly excellence, the Department of Humanities maintains a distinguished and influential presence
within the academic community.

# Summary

The Department of Musicology and Cultural Heritage, located in Cremona, Italy, is a leading European center for
musicological studies and cultural heritage research. Established in 1952, the department fosters an interdisciplinary
approach to research, encompassing musicology, art history, literary studies, and the conservation and restoration of
cultural assets. It actively engages in national and international research projects, publishes scholarly works, and
organizes conferences. The department also plays a significant role in cultural dissemination and public engagement,
managing notable collections such as historical musical instruments and the Ugo Tognazzi Archive, and promoting open

# General Description

The Department of Musicology and Cultural Heritage is an academic unit dedicated to advanced research and education in
the fields of musicology and cultural heritage. With a history dating back to the establishment of the School of Musical
Paleography in Cremona in 1952, the department has evolved into a prominent European hub for these disciplines. Its
research activities are characterized by a strong interdisciplinary focus, integrating various humanistic and scientific
methodologies to explore diverse themes.

The department is actively involved in numerous national and international research projects, contributing significantly
to scholarly discourse through its publications, including two musicological series and the online journal "Philomusica
on-line." Beyond academic research, the department is committed to the preservation and valorization of cultural assets.
It houses a significant collection of historical musical instruments and manages the Ugo Tognazzi Archive, dedicated to
the legacy of the renowned Italian actor and director.

In addition to its core research and educational missions, the Department of Musicology and Cultural Heritage is deeply
engaged with the local community and broader society. It undertakes various public engagement initiatives aimed at
cultural dissemination, continuing education, and fostering dialogue between different cultural traditions. These
activities include organizing conferences, seminars, and public events, as well as providing training for professionals
in related fields. The department also champions open science practices, developing innovative tools to support the
accessibility and sharing of knowledge.

# Summary

The Department of Internal Medicine and Medical Therapy was established in 1982 to unite clinicians and researchers with
shared scientific and educational interests, fostering broad-ranging projects from basic sciences to medical
specialties. Over the years, it has expanded to include 12 sections/units, focusing on personalized medicine through the
study of biological and molecular bases of pathologies. This approach aims to determine individual disease
predisposition, develop targeted preventive strategies, and create innovative diagnostic systems and effective
individualized treatments. The department's research activities are characterized by an interdisciplinary approach,
leveraging diverse expertise and collaborations with local IRCCS and other hospital structures.

# General Description

The Department of Internal Medicine and Medical Therapy, founded in 1982, serves as a hub for clinicians and researchers
dedicated to advancing medical knowledge and practice. Its initial mission was to aggregate groups with common
scientific and didactic interests, enabling the development of comprehensive projects spanning from fundamental
scientific principles to specialized medical applications. This collaborative environment has fostered significant
scientific growth, promoting cross-disciplinary approaches and the sharing of expertise among its members.

Over time, the department has expanded considerably, now encompassing 12 distinct sections or units and a substantial
number of faculty and researchers. This growth has been driven by the continuous integration of new academics based on
their scientific and teaching merits. The interaction among groups with diverse specializations and backgrounds has
facilitated an optimization of research activities, particularly through the exchange of technologies and experiences.

A central focus of the department's research agenda is personalized medicine. This innovative model involves a deep
investigation into the biological and molecular underpinnings of various pathologies. The ultimate goals are to
accurately identify individual predispositions to specific diseases, formulate highly targeted preventive strategies,
and engineer novel diagnostic tools and individualized therapeutic interventions. This ambitious research plan is
realized through strategic partnerships with local Scientific Institutes for Research, Hospitalization and Healthcare (
IRCCS) and other hospital facilities, further enriched by the positive heterogeneity of its research groups.

The department's competencies span numerous areas of medicine and medical therapy, consistently integrating the
biological bases of diseases. This comprehensive approach is evidenced by a prolific output of scientific publications
in high-impact journals, active participation in national and international societies, and representation on the
editorial boards of esteemed scientific periodicals. The department's clinical prestige and scientific experience are
further underscored by its involvement in international multicenter clinical trials, often serving as national
coordinating centers, with results published in leading clinical journals.

the comprehensive study of the brain and its functions. Its activities span clinical neurosciences, neurophysiology,

# General Description

The Department of Neurosciences and Behavior (DBBS) is an academic unit committed to advancing scientific understanding
of the nervous system and behavior through rigorous research and comprehensive educational programs. Established in
2013, the department integrates various disciplines to explore the intricate workings of the brain and its impact on
human actions and mental processes. Its core mission encompasses scientific inquiry, didactic activities, and external
training initiatives, all centered on the study of the brain and its functions.

The department is structured into three main sections: Biostatistics, Neurophysiology and Psychiatry; Clinical
Neurosciences; and Psychology. Within the Biostatistics, Neurophysiology and Psychiatry section, research focuses on the
neural underpinnings of cognitive functions in both healthy and diseased states, utilizing neurophysiological and
neurophysiopathological basic research, modeling, medical and genomic statistics, and clinical psychiatry.

The Clinical Neurosciences section is dedicated to high-quality translational research involving adult, child, and
adolescent patients. This research aims to identify, describe, and comprehend the fundamental mechanisms that govern
typical and atypical, physiological and pathological neurological functioning in humans. Key areas include
neurodiagnostics, clinical and rehabilitative neurology, and child neuropsychiatry.

The Psychology section operates as an innovative multidisciplinary center, with research directed at understanding the
psychological and neural processes that drive both normal and pathological behavior. This includes general psychology,
neuropsychology and cognitive neurosciences, developmental psychology, social psychology, organizational psychology, and
dynamic and clinical psychology. The department's holistic approach ensures a broad and deep exploration of the complex
interplay between the brain, behavior, and mental health.
