# Summary

The Department of Electrical, Computer and Biomedical Engineering is an academic unit focused on engineering, driving
scientific and technological progress through education, innovation, and technology transfer. Its research encompasses a
wide array of fields, including health technology, bioengineering, artificial intelligence, telecommunications,
microelectronics, and robotics, aiming to address contemporary societal challenges.

# General Description

The Department of Electrical, Computer and Biomedical Engineering is a prominent academic unit dedicated to advancing
scientific and technological progress in engineering. It serves as a driving force for education, innovation, and
technology transfer, addressing contemporary challenges and opportunities. The department is located at the University
Campus Cravino in Pavia, Italy.

The department's research activities span a broad spectrum of engineering disciplines, with a strong emphasis on
interdisciplinary approaches. Key areas of focus include health technology, bioengineering, bioinformatics, and
information technology. Researchers engage in projects related to advanced telecommunications, artificial intelligence,
data science, and the automotive sector.

Further specializations encompass microelectronics and photonics, the Internet of Things (IoT), embedded systems, and
remote sensing. The department also conducts significant work in imaging and multimedia, mechatronics, numerical
modeling for engineering science, energy systems, high-performance computing, robotics, and industrial automation. This
diverse research portfolio aims to generate new knowledge and develop innovative solutions across various critical
sectors.

In addition to research, the department is committed to high-quality education, offering bachelor's and master's degree
programs in areas such as Bioengineering, Electronic Engineering, and Computer Engineering. It also hosts PhD programs
in Bioengineering, Bioinformatics, and Health Technologies, fostering the next generation of engineers and researchers.
