# Summary

The Department of Biology and Biotechnology "L. Spallanzani" (DBB) is a dynamic research unit focused on advancing
scientific knowledge in biological and biotechnological fields. It conducts diverse research activities, ranging from
molecular and cellular biology to genomics, neurobiology, and environmental applications. The department is committed to
innovation, knowledge transfer, and public engagement, fostering collaborations with industry and promoting the
dissemination of research findings.

# General Description

The Department of Biology and Biotechnology "L. Spallanzani" (DBB) is a prominent research institution dedicated to
exploring fundamental biological processes and developing innovative biotechnological applications. The department
encompasses a broad spectrum of scientific inquiry, utilizing advanced methodologies in areas such as bioinformatics,
genomics, and molecular biology. Its research endeavors contribute to understanding complex biological systems,
addressing human health challenges, and developing sustainable solutions in agriculture and environmental science.

The DBB is recognized for its commitment to excellence in research, as evidenced by its designation as a "Department of
Excellence" in the biological area for the quinquennium 2018-2022. Beyond its core research activities, the department
actively engages in knowledge transfer and public engagement initiatives. This includes fostering collaborations with
industrial partners, managing patents and spin-off companies, and disseminating scientific knowledge to a wider
audience. The department also plays a significant role in academic training, offering various courses and programs in
biological and biotechnological sciences.
